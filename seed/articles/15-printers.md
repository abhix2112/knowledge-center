All office printers use **PrintHub** follow-me printing: you print to one queue, then release the job at any printer by tapping your badge.

## Add PrintHub

1. Connect to HALDEN-STAFF or the VPN.
2. Open Company Portal and install **PrintHub Queue**.
3. Print to **Halden-PrintHub** from any app.
4. At a printer, tap your badge and choose your jobs.

Jobs that aren't released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Common errors

| Printer shows | Meaning | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Error 49.4C02 | Printer firmware error | Turn the printer off for 30 seconds and on again |
| Badge not recognised | Your badge isn't linked yet | Release jobs with your PIN, and ask reception to link your badge |
| Job not in the list | You printed to a direct printer, not PrintHub | Print again to Halden-PrintHub |
| Paper jam in tray 2 | Paper jam | Follow the animation on the printer screen |

Report a printer that's still broken in the Service Portal, with the printer's asset tag (on the front label).
