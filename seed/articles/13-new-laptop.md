Your laptop arrives ready to use. These steps take about 30 minutes on your first day.

1. **Sign in** with your Halden email and the temporary password from your welcome email. You'll be asked to choose a new one.
2. **Set up MFA**: see *Set up multi-factor authentication*.
3. **Connect to HALDEN-STAFF** Wi-Fi in the office. It connects automatically.
4. **Open Company Portal** and wait until it shows "All apps installed". Outlook, Teams, GlobalConnect and Chrome install automatically.
5. **Sign in to OneDrive.** Your Desktop and Documents folders back up automatically from now on.
6. **Add a printer**: see *Connect to an office printer*.
7. **Test the VPN** once, so you know it works before you need it from home.

Laptops are replaced every 4 years. Don't install software from outside Company Portal; see *Install approved software*.
