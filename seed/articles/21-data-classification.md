Every document and dataset at Halden has one of four labels. The label decides where it can be stored and who it can be shared with.

| Label | Examples | Can be shared with |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Website content, press releases | Anyone |
| **Internal** | Org charts, most knowledge articles, policies | Halden staff and contractors |
| **Confidential** | Customer contracts, shipment data, pricing | Only people who need it for their work |
| **Restricted** | Salaries, health data, passwords and keys | Named people only, encrypted at all times |

## Rules

- Unlabelled documents count as **Internal**.
- Confidential and Restricted data must stay in Halden systems (OneDrive, SharePoint, approved apps). Never in personal email or personal cloud storage.
- Don't paste Confidential or Restricted data into public AI tools. Use the approved internal assistant.

Apply a label in Office apps with **Home → Sensitivity**.
