Report a lost or stolen company device **immediately**, even if you think you'll find it. Fast reporting lets us protect company data.

## What to do

1. Call the Service Desk (24/7) or open an urgent ticket from another device.
2. Tell us what was lost, when and where you last had it.
3. If it was stolen, report it to the police and send us the report number.

## What we do

- Lock the device remotely and sign it out of every company app.
- If it isn't recovered within 48 hours, or it was stolen, **wipe** it remotely.
- Order a replacement. Your OneDrive files are safe and will be on the new device.

Personal phones with Halden email: we remove only company data (email, Teams), never your personal photos or apps.
