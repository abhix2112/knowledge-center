Employees who work from home at least 2 days a week can claim a one-time **home office allowance of 600 EUR**, renewed every 3 years.

## What it covers

- Desk and ergonomic chair
- Monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset
- Desk lamp

Laptops, docking stations and chargers are provided by IT and don't come out of the allowance.

## How to claim

1. Buy the equipment yourself and keep the receipts.
2. Submit them in the expense tool within 60 days, category **Home office**. See *Submit travel and expense receipts*.
3. Your manager approves, and the amount is paid with your next salary.

Equipment bought with the allowance is yours to keep, also if you leave Halden.
