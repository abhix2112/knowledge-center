After **10 failed sign-in attempts in 15 minutes**, your account locks for 30 minutes. This protects you from password-guessing attacks.

## Why it happens to people who know their password

The usual cause is a device still using an **old password**: a phone's mail app, a second laptop, or a mapped drive. It keeps retrying in the background and locks you out again.

## What to do

1. Wait 30 minutes; the lock clears itself. Or reset your password at `id.halden.example`, which also unlocks the account.
2. Update the password on every device: phone mail, tablets, any other computer.
3. If you're locked out again within an hour, call the Service Desk. They can see which device is sending the failed attempts.

The Service Desk will never ask for your password, by phone or by email.
