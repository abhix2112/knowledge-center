Every Halden office has two Wi-Fi networks.

## HALDEN-STAFF

For company laptops only. It connects automatically using your laptop's certificate; you never type a password. On it, you can reach internal systems without the VPN.

If a company laptop doesn't connect automatically, its certificate may have expired. Connect to HALDEN-GUEST, open Company Portal and click **Sync**, then restart.

## HALDEN-GUEST

For visitors and personal phones. The password changes every Monday and is shown on the screens at reception. Guest devices can reach the internet only, not internal systems.

Don't connect company laptops to HALDEN-GUEST except to fix the certificate as described above.

## Warehouses

Warehouse floors use HALDEN-SCAN for handheld scanners. Laptops can't join it. Use HALDEN-STAFF in the warehouse offices.
