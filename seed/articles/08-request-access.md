Access to shared drives and business applications is granted per team and approved by the resource owner.

## How to request

1. Open the Service Portal and choose **Request access**.
2. Pick the drive or application. If it isn't listed, choose **Other** and give its name or path.
3. Explain why you need it and for how long.
4. Submit. The request goes to the owner of the resource for approval.

## How long it takes

- Standard requests: usually within 1 business day after approval.
- Finance and HR systems need a second approval from your manager, so allow 2 to 3 days.

## Leaving a team

Access from your old team is removed 30 days after a team change. If you still need something, request it again with a reason.
