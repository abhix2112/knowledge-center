## Share your calendar

1. In Outlook, open **Calendar** and right-click your calendar → **Sharing permissions**.
2. Add the person and choose a level:
   - **Can view when I'm busy**: free/busy only. Everyone at Halden has this by default.
   - **Can view titles and locations**
   - **Can view all details**
3. They get an email with a link to add your calendar.

## Give an assistant (delegate) access

Delegates can accept meetings and send invitations on your behalf.

1. Right-click your calendar → **Sharing permissions**.
2. Add the person and choose **Delegate**.
3. Choose whether they receive copies of meeting requests.

Private appointments stay hidden from delegates unless you allow it.
