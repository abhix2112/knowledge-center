## Won't charge

1. Try a different power socket and check the charger's light.
2. Unplug the charger from both ends for 30 seconds, then reconnect.
3. On USB-C laptops, try the other USB-C port. Only some ports charge.
4. If the battery icon shows "Plugged in, not charging", restart the laptop.

If it still doesn't charge, request a replacement charger in the Service Portal. Spare chargers are available at every office reception.

## Drains fast

- Check **Settings → Battery** for apps using the most power. Browser tabs with video are a common cause.
- Lower the screen brightness and turn on battery saver.
- Batteries older than 3 years hold noticeably less charge. If your battery health is below 70%, request a battery replacement.

**A swollen battery** (the case bulges or the trackpad is hard to click) is a safety risk. Stop using the laptop, unplug it, and contact the Service Desk right away.
