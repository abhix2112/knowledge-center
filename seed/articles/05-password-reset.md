You can reset your Halden password yourself, any time, at **id.halden.example**.

## Steps

1. Go to `id.halden.example` and click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your Halden email.
3. Approve the request in the Authenticator app. If you can't, use one of your backup codes.
4. Choose a new password.

## Password rules

- At least 14 characters. A passphrase like `copper-lantern-river-9` works well.
- Not one of your last 10 passwords.
- Passwords don't expire on a schedule. Change yours only if you think someone knows it.

## After resetting

Your laptop, Outlook and phone may keep asking for the old password for a few minutes. Lock and unlock your laptop while connected to HALDEN-STAFF or the VPN so it picks up the new one. If Outlook keeps prompting, see *Outlook keeps asking for my password*.

If the self-service reset doesn't work, see *Account locked after too many sign-in attempts*.
