Every mailbox has **50 GB** of storage, plus an **online archive** that grows automatically.

## What happens near the limit

| Mailbox size | What happens |
|---|---|
| 45 GB | Warning email |
| 49 GB | You can't send email |
| 50 GB | You can't send or receive email |

## The archive

Email older than **2 years** moves to your online archive automatically. It still appears in Outlook under **Online Archive** and is fully searchable. Nothing is deleted.

## Free up space quickly

- Sort the **Sent Items** and **Inbox** folders by size and delete large attachments you have elsewhere.
- Empty **Deleted Items**.
- Share files as OneDrive links instead of attachments.

This policy replaces the previous 10 GB limit.
