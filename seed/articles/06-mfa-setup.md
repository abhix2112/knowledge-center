Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every Halden account. When you sign in, you approve a notification on your phone, so a stolen password alone isn't enough to get in.

## Set it up

1. Install **Microsoft Authenticator** on your phone.
2. On your laptop, go to `id.halden.example/security`.
3. Click **Add method → Authenticator app** and scan the QR code with the app.
4. Approve the test notification.
5. Download your **backup codes** and keep them somewhere safe, not on your laptop.

## New phone?

Set up the app on the new phone **before** wiping the old one: add the new phone at `id.halden.example/security`, then remove the old one. If you've already lost the old phone, use a backup code to sign in, or call the Service Desk to verify your identity.

## Unexpected approval requests

If you get an approval request you didn't trigger, **deny it** and report it. Someone may have your password. See *Spot and report phishing emails*.
