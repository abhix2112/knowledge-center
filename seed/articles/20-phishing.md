Phishing emails try to trick you into giving away your password, approving an MFA request, or opening malware.

## Warning signs

- Urgency: "Your account will be closed in 24 hours".
- A sender address that's almost right: `halden-it@hald3n.com`.
- Links whose real address (hover to see it) doesn't match the text.
- Unexpected attachments, especially `.zip`, `.html` or Office files asking you to "enable content".
- Requests for gift cards, payment changes or passwords. Halden IT never asks for your password.

## Report it

Click **Report phishing** in Outlook's toolbar. The email is sent to the security team and removed from your inbox. Don't forward it, and don't click anything in it.

## Already clicked?

Report it to the Service Desk **right away**, then reset your password at `id.halden.example`. Clicking is not a disciplinary issue; reporting late is what makes damage worse.
