Draft - pending legal review. Not yet in effect.

Every parent gets **20 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, regardless of gender or how they became a parent, to be taken within 18 months of the birth or adoption.

- Leave can be split into up to 3 blocks.
- Tell your manager and HR at least 8 weeks before your leave starts.
- Returning parents can work 80% hours on full pay for the first 4 weeks back.
