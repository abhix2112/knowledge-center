Each mailbox has a limit of **10 GB**.

- At 9 GB you receive a warning.
- At 10 GB you can't send or receive email.

There is no automatic archive. To free up space, export older email to a PST file on your laptop: **File → Open & Export → Import/Export → Export to a file**. Keep PST files in your Documents folder so they are backed up.
