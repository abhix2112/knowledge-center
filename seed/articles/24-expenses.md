Submit expenses in the **Spendly** app (mobile or web) within **30 days** of the purchase.

## Steps

1. Take a photo of the receipt in Spendly, or forward e-receipts to `receipts@halden.example`.
2. Choose a category (Travel, Meals, Home office, Training).
3. For meals with others, list the attendees.
4. Submit. Your manager approves, then Finance pays you within 10 business days.

## Limits

| Expense | Limit |
|---|---|
| Hotel per night | 180 EUR (250 EUR in capital cities) |
| Meals per day while travelling | 60 EUR |
| Train | Second class; first class for journeys over 3 hours |
| Flights | Economy; book through the travel portal |

Expenses submitted after 90 days can't be reimbursed.
