SecureLink 3 is the VPN client for reaching internal systems from home or while travelling.

## Connect

1. Open **SecureLink 3** from the system tray.
2. Choose the gateway `remote.halden.example`.
3. Enter your Halden username (not your email) and password.
4. Enter the 6-digit code from your hardware token.
5. Click **Connect**. The tray icon turns blue when connected.

## Good to know

- Sessions disconnect after 8 hours.
- All traffic, including video calls, goes through the VPN. Close Teams calls before connecting if your connection is slow.
- Hardware tokens are issued by the Service Desk. Report a lost token immediately.
